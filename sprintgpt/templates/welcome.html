What you'll get

Fitness & form

Track fitness (CTL), fatigue (ATL), and form (TSB) so you know when to push and when to recover.

Heart‑rate zones

Five‑zone breakdowns and per‑run time‑in‑zone, with TRIMP training load when HR is available.

Race predictions

Realistic, progressive 5K → marathon predictions grounded in your own runs and endurance — they improve as you build up, never fantasy times.

Exact splits

Turn one goal time into precise per‑mile and per‑km splits for even, confident pacing.

Personalized plans

Base → Build → Peak → Taper plans built around your goal race and current fitness.
